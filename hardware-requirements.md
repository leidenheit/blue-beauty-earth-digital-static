# Requirements

{: .lead}
Blue Beauty Earth Digital is a watch face for Garmin watches with a round AMOLED
or MicroLED display. This page lists what it needs and what each feature
depends on, so you know before you buy.

## At a glance

- **A supported watch** from the list below, with its current software.
- **The Garmin Connect app on your phone**, to install the face from the
  Connect IQ Store and to change its settings. The phone also brings the
  weather and your notifications to the watch.
- **No additional hardware.** You don't need a chest strap, an external sensor
  or a companion app. Everything the face shows comes from the watch itself or
  through Garmin Connect.
- **A touchscreen is a plus, not a must.** It adds two shortcuts: holding a
  data field opens the matching widget of your watch, and the on-watch color
  picker follows your finger. Every setting also works with the buttons.

## What each feature needs

### On every supported watch

- The animated Earth with its day and night side, the time in large digits,
  the seconds comet that runs over the Earth, the date line and the status row
  with the battery.
- The three round data fields and the three status arcs, on the right or the
  left of the time as you choose.
- The data fields steps, calories, heart rate, distance, respiration,
  Pulse Ox, Body Battery, stress, intensity minutes, step goal, move bar and
  battery.

### A touchscreen

Every supported watch has one except the Instinct® 3 AMOLED.

- **Hold to open:** hold a round data field or the icon of a status arc for
  about a second, and the watch opens its own widget for that data: steps,
  heart rate, Body Battery, stress, intensity minutes, the weather and more.
  Distance and the move bar have no widget of their own; holding them, or any
  other part of the face, does what your watch does with a hold anyway. A field
  you have switched off reacts to nothing, and neither does the always-on look.
- **The color picker by touch:** every color setting on the watch opens a live
  preview of the face with the palette along the edge. Swipe or drag anywhere to
  move through the colors, tap a dot to jump to it, and tap the color's name in
  the middle to keep it. With the buttons: UP and DOWN to move, START to keep,
  BACK to leave without a change.

### Your phone, through Garmin Connect

- **Weather temperature** and the **weather symbol** of the always-on look. The
  watch gets the weather from the paired phone. Without a recent connection
  these stay empty.
- **Sunrise / sunset** needs a location. It comes from the weather data or,
  without weather, from the watch's last known GPS position (for example from
  your last outdoor activity). Until one of them exists, the field stays empty.
- **Notification bell**: shown while unread phone notifications are waiting on
  the watch.

### Your watch settings

- **Always-on look**: the watch's own *Display > Always On* setting decides
  whether it appears. The face has no switch of its own for it.
- **Night Earth and the warm night colors** follow your sleep. The face uses
  the watch's sleep mode, or else the sleep and wake times from your Garmin
  user profile.
- **Pulse Ox** shows a value once the watch has measured one. You set when it
  measures in the watch's Pulse Ox settings.
- **Wheelchair pushes and distance** count only while wheelchair mode is on in
  your user profile, and only on the watches marked in the table.
- **Units, °C / °F and 12 / 24 hours** follow the watch by default. You can fix
  them in the face's settings.

## Permissions and privacy

When you install the face, the watch asks you to allow these permissions:

| Permission | What the face uses it for |
|---|---|
| Complications | Opening your watch's own widget for a data field when you hold it, and the latest Pulse Ox reading. |
| Positioning | The last known position, and only to calculate sunrise and sunset when the weather data brings no location. |
| Sensor history | The latest Body Battery, stress, Pulse Ox, elevation and air-pressure readings. |
| User profile | Your sleep and wake times, for the night Earth and the night colors. |

The face has **no internet access**: it doesn't request the communications
permission. It collects, stores and sends no personal data. Every value is read
on the watch and shown only there. Garmin Connect and the watch keep the
settings you choose, as they do for any watch face.

## The turning Earth

<!-- animation:begin - generated by tools/make_pages_devices.py in the watch face repo, do not edit -->

The Earth is a pre-rendered animation of **120 frames at 10 frames per second, 12 seconds long**. In it the Earth turns by **30°**, about 2.5° per second, and the cloud layer drifts faster, 73.2° over the same 12 seconds.

- It starts again from the same position every time the screen lights up. It plays only while the screen is fully lit, and the watch decides how long that is (usually a few seconds after you raise your wrist). So you usually see the first part of the 30° turn, never a full revolution.
- It is not a live globe. It shows neither the current time of day nor the real position of the sun. The night side is its own animation, shown while you sleep.
- In the always-on look the Earth is a still image drawn in fine dots of light: an even arc for its rim, dense dots on the continents, sparse ones over the oceans. On the night side the city lights shine in shades from light gold to warm white, and with the warm night colors on, the Earth takes the same warm tone as the time and the date. With the animation switched off in the settings, the lit face shows the first frame as a still image.

## Battery

The animation and the seconds comet run only while the screen is fully lit.
Battery life depends mostly on the watch's own *Always On* setting.
